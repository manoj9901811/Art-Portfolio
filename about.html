WHO AM I

Creating Art That Lives Forever

Welcome to Manoj's Art, where creativity meets craftsmanship. Every artwork is carefully handcrafted to capture emotions, preserve memories, and transform ideas into timeless masterpieces.

What started as a passion for sketching has evolved into a journey of creating paintings, mandala art, pen art, and custom portraits that bring joy to people and beauty to their spaces.

Every commission is treated as a unique story. Whether it's a gift for a loved one, a personalized portrait, or a decorative masterpiece, every stroke is made with patience, precision, and dedication.

MY JOURNEY

The Artistic Timeline

Every artwork tells a story, and every year has been a step toward turning passion into a lifelong artistic journey.

2020

Beginning My Artistic Journey

My passion for art began with pencil sketches and simple drawings. Every day became an opportunity to learn, practice, and improve my artistic skills.

2022

Exploring New Art Forms

I expanded my creativity by exploring different art styles, including paintings, mandala art, pen art, and realistic sketches. During this time, I also created my Instagram page to share my artwork with the world.

2024

Creating Memories Through Art

I dedicated myself to creating meaningful handmade gifts and personalized artworks for my loved ones, gaining valuable experience in custom art and portrait commissions.

2026

Launching Manoj's Art

I am preparing to officially launch Manoj's Art, creating a platform where people can explore my artwork and commission unique handmade creations crafted with passion and creativity.
